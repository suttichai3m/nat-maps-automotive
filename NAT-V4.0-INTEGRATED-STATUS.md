Monthly EV archive starts after install; previous missing days not backfilled. Export private JSON; no network upload. No destructive reset supplied yet; max 365 shifts. Driver view is optional and keeps SOC cards. No passenger names needed.

No new car SDK signal guessed. Previous V3.7 31-point status remains in NAT-V3.7-MASTER-STATUS.md.
